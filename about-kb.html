About Khushi Baby

Founded in 2014 at Yale University. Now a 120-member team embedded within government health systems across India.

Our Story

From a Yale classroom to India’s public health infrastructure

In 2014, an interdisciplinary team of students at Yale prototyped a wearable immunization tracker for newborns in Udaipur, Rajasthan. What started as a hardware project evolved into something more fundamental: the insight that India’s public health system doesn’t need more data collection — it needs data that connects, informs, and drives action.

Today, Khushi Baby operates as a technical partner to three state governments, powering one of the world’s largest community health worker platforms. The organization has grown from two founders to a 120-member team, from one village to 40,000, and from a proof of concept to a model being considered for national adoption.

Mission

Strengthen public health systems to complete care feedback loops at the last mile — ensuring no high-risk mother or child falls through the cracks.

Vision

A world where every community health system learns, improves, and delivers with precision.
